01 / PROFILE

A research-driven engineering perspective.

My engineering interests are centered on the connection between electromagnetic behavior and the information carried through physical systems. I am especially interested in understanding not only whether a design works, but why it works, where its limits are, and how reliably it can be reproduced.

This perspective guides my approach to RF subsystems, receiver architectures, antenna development, embedded electronics, and emerging computational electromagnetic concepts. I work across theoretical analysis, MATLAB/Python modeling, full-wave electromagnetic simulation, circuit/PCB design, and system integration—selecting the tools appropriate to the problem rather than the other way around.

The projects on this website document different stages of engineering maturity. Some are validated through software or numerical experiments, some through hardware and system integration, and others are ongoing research concepts. I aim to distinguish these stages clearly.
