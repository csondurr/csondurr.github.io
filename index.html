03 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

Theory is the starting point. Evidence is the standard.

The value of a technical result depends on its assumptions, reproducibility, and the way it was verified. I use that principle to organize my design and research work.

  1. 01

    Formulate

    Define the physical problem, requirements, assumptions, and measurable objectives.

  2. 02

    Model & Design

    Develop mathematical models, simulations, algorithms, circuits, and architectures.

  3. 03

    Evaluate

    Use appropriate numerical tests, software validation, and hardware measurements where available.

  4. 04

    Document

    Present parameters, results, trade-offs, limitations, and the next verification steps.
