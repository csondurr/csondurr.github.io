About Me

I am an Electrical and Electronics Engineer focused on RF and microwave systems, antenna engineering, digital signal processing, SDR/FPGA architectures, and advanced wireless technologies. I combine electromagnetic theory, simulation, prototyping, and validation to develop research-driven solutions for communication, sensing, and electronic-warfare applications.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To contribute original, high-impact engineering to next-generation communication, sensing, and defense systems by advancing RF, microwave, antenna, and signal-processing technologies.

Mission

To turn complex electromagnetic and signal problems into validated, reproducible solutions through disciplined analysis, simulation, implementation, and clear technical documentation.
